This is the start-presentation skill at work. The repository's rule is that a storyboard comes before slides, and that you read it.

If the agent builds slides at once, say: "Stop. Storyboard first: one row per slide, what each key press shows. Show it to me." That it should not happen is the point of the instruction files; if it does, your agent may not be reading them.

Any topic works. Three slides keep it short.
