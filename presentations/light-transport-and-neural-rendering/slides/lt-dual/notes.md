Helmholtz reciprocity: a light path reversed has the same throughput.

- Click 1: Sen et al.'s diagram. In the primal configuration, a projector shows pattern $p'$ and a camera records $c' = \mathbf{T}p'$. Swap them: a virtual projector at the camera and a virtual camera at the projector. The transport matrix of the swapped setup is $\mathbf{T}^\top$, so $p'' = \mathbf{T}^\top c''$. Once $\mathbf{T}$ is measured, an image from the projector's position is $\mathbf{T}^\top c''$.
- Click 2: their card example. The camera's view shows only the back of a playing card and a book page. The projector lights the card's face; light from the face reaches the book page and then the camera. From the measured $\mathbf{T}$, the dual image shows the card's face, the king of hearts, although no camera imaged it directly. The dual image has 66 × 87 pixels, one per projector block.

To remember: the transpose of the transport matrix is transport with light source and sensor exchanged. The adjoint section uses the same transpose.
