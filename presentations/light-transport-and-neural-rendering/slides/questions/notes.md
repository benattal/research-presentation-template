Back-up answers:

- Why does the Neumann series converge for light? Every surface absorbs some of the light it receives (its albedo is below one), so the norm of the transport operator $A$ is below one, and $\rho(A) \le \|A\| < 1$.
- Why is the transpose the right adjoint? Under Helmholtz reciprocity the BRDF is symmetric in its two directions, so $S^\top$ is scattering with the directions swapped, and $G^\top$ is propagation along the same rays in the opposite direction.
- Where do I start reading? Veach's thesis (chapters 3 and 4) for operators, importance, and adjoints; Arvo's thesis (chapter 6) for the operator view and its norms; the appendix slides of this deck for the large-system iteration, the three-point form, and more computational imaging.
