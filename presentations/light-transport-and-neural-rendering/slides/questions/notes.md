If asked:
- Why does the Neumann series converge for light? Every surface absorbs some of the light it receives, so the norm of the transport operator $A$ is below 1, and $\rho(A) \le \|A\| < 1$.
- Why is the transpose the right adjoint? Under Helmholtz reciprocity, the BRDF is symmetric in its two directions. So $S^\top$ is scattering with the directions swapped, and $G^\top$ is propagation along the same rays in the opposite direction.
- Where do I start reading? Veach's thesis, chapters 3 and 4, for operators, importance, and adjoints; Arvo's thesis, chapter 6, for the operator view and its norms; and the appendix slides of this deck.
