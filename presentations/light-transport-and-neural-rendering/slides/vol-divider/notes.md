Back to the main thread, after the aside. Between surfaces, radiance was constant along a ray: light changed only where it met a surface. In fog, smoke, clouds, or skin, light is absorbed and scattered all along the ray.

This section writes that as an equation, the radiative transfer equation, and brings it to the fixed-point form of the surfaces section, $x = b + Ax$, with a new operator $A$: one scattering event in the medium. Then it drops scattering, which leaves the model that neural radiance fields use, and computes that model from samples along each ray.
