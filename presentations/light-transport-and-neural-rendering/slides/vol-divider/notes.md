Back to the main thread. In fog, smoke, skin, or clouds, light is absorbed and scattered along the whole ray, not only at surfaces: the volumetric paths of the light transport slide in the aside.

$x$ is radiance in the medium, $b$ the emission plus attenuated light from surfaces, and $A$ in-scattering weighted by transmittance. The equation has the form $x = b + Ax$ again, with a new operator.
