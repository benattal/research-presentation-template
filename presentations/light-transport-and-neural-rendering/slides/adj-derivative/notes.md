Here $\theta = \rho$, the albedo of the red wall.

- Step 1: the light $x$, the solution of $x = e + Ax$.
- Step 2: differentiate both sides: $\partial_\rho x = (\partial_\rho A)\, x + A\, \partial_\rho x$.
- Step 3: the source term $(\partial_\rho A)\, x$ is the extra light leaving the red wall when its albedo increases. It is nonzero only on the wall.
- Step 4: solve: $\partial_\rho x = (I - A)^{-1} (\partial_\rho A)\, x$. The derivative of the light is transported by the same operator as light, from a source on the wall.
