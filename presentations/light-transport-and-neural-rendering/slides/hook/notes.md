Start with a picture before any equation.

- Click 1, direct illumination: light that went from the light source to a surface and then to the camera. The shadows are black, because no light reaches them directly.
- Click 2, indirect illumination: light that bounced at least twice. The shadows fill in, and the red carpet tints the walls.
- Click 3, the sum. In a photograph both are present: each pixel is a sum of light over every path that reaches it.

These are renders of a church interior from Gkioulekas's CMU 15-468 lecture, adapted from Wojciech Jarosz.

To remember: a pixel is a sum over light paths. The rest of the lecture writes that sum as an equation, then solves, measures, and differentiates it.
