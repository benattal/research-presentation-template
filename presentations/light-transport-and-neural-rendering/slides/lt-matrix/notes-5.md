- Step 6: The rest of the series adds the three-bounce paths, and each element's glow becomes its whole entry $\mathbf{T}_{jk}$. Most of the light arrives along one-bounce paths, from the elements nearest the floor point.

Remember: a column of $\mathbf{T}$ is the image under one light; a row is the light from every source, along every path, into one pixel.
