Two facts the rendering equation needs.

- Click 1, beam: one beam from the spotlight. In empty space, radiance is the same at every point along a ray. The beam is narrow and points one way.
- Clicks 2–4: the floor's BRDF describes where the reflected light goes. From a diffuse floor, light leaves in every direction; from a glossy floor, in a lobe around the mirror direction; from a mirror, in a single beam toward the ceiling.

The numbers are the check: the fraction of the beam's power that leaves the floor. It is 77% for all three, the floor's albedo. Where the reflected light goes depends on the BRDF; how much of it there is depends on the albedo.
