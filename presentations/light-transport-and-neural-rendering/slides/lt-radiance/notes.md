Transition: an appendix slide, for a question about radiance or BRDFs. It shows the two facts behind propagation $G$ and scattering $S$.

- Step 1: One beam from a spotlight on the left wall. In empty space, radiance is constant along a ray: the beam has the same brightness from the wall to the floor. So propagation $G$ only maps the light leaving one surface to the light arriving at the next.
- Step 2: From a diffuse floor, the reflected light leaves in every direction.
- Step 3: From a glossy floor, in a lobe around the mirror direction.
- Step 4: From a mirror, in a single beam, toward the ceiling. The column is the fraction of the beam's power reflected: 77% for all three, the floor's albedo.

Remember: radiance changes only at surfaces, where the BRDF sets the directions of the reflected light and the albedo its amount.
