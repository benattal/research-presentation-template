An aside before volumes: the same operators, used in the lab instead of a renderer.

With a projector as the light source and a camera as the sensor, light transport is a matrix $\mathbf{T}$ from projector pixels to camera pixels. Computational imaging measures parts of $\mathbf{T}$, applies it with light instead of a computer, and separates its components (direct and global light). These slides are mostly results; the main thread continues with volume rendering.
