An aside before volumes: the same operators, used in the lab instead of a renderer.

With a projector as the light source and a camera as the sensor, light transport is a matrix $\mathbf{T}$ from projector pixels to camera pixels. In computational imaging, parts of $\mathbf{T}$ are measured, products with it are computed optically, and its direct and global parts are separated. These slides are mostly results; the main thread continues with volume rendering.
