Applying $A$ again and again gives the bounces one at a time: $A^k e$ is the light that has bounced exactly $k$ times.

- $e$: light leaving the lamp.
- $Ae$: direct illumination.
- $A^2 e$: the first indirect bounce, colored by the walls.
- $A^3 e$, $A^4 e$: each bounce keeps about two thirds of the light of the one before, because every albedo is below one.

All frames use the same exposure, so the dimming is real.
