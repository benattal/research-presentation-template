Question to the class: what is $(I - A)\,S_k$? Give them a moment to multiply it out.

Answer: $(I - A)S_k = S_k - AS_k = (I + A + \cdots + A^k) - (A + \cdots + A^k + A^{k+1}) = I - A^{k+1}$. Each power from $A$ to $A^k$ appears once with each sign and cancels; only $I$ and $-A^{k+1}$ remain. It is the identity $(1 - a)(1 + a + \cdots + a^k) = 1 - a^{k+1}$ for a number $a$, with $A$ in its place.
