Welcome. The lecture is built on one linear equation, $x = e + Ax$, from linear algebra through light transport, volume rendering, and neural rendering, to the adjoint method for differentiating it.

The picture is the room used on every interactive slide: a two-dimensional Cornell box with a lamp in the ceiling, a red and a green wall, and a box. It is a solution of the light transport equation, with soft shadows and color bleeding from the walls.
