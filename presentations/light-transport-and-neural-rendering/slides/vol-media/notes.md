Transition: A volume that absorbs, scatters, or emits light is called a participating medium. Here are four.

- Step 1: Fog and clouds are water droplets, which scatter light; the shafts in the fog are sunlight scattered toward the camera. Smoke is soot, which mostly absorbs. In jade, light enters the stone, scatters inside, and leaves somewhere else, as in skin, marble, or milk.

Remember: in a medium, light can be absorbed, scattered, or emitted at every point along a ray.

Stop (optional): the cloud and the smoke are both small particles in air. Why is one white and the other dark? Answer: a droplet scatters almost all the light it meets, and soot absorbs most of it.

If asked:
- What is subsurface scattering? Light that enters a solid, scatters inside, and leaves elsewhere, as in the jade.
