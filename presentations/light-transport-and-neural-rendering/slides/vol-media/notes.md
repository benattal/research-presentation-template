Transition: so far light changed only at surfaces; between surfaces radiance was constant along a ray. In a participating medium that is no longer true.

- Fog and clouds: water droplets scatter light. The shafts in the fog are light scattered toward the camera from the sunlit parts of the air.
- Smoke: soot mostly absorbs, so the plume is dark.
- Jade: a solid can be a medium too. Light enters the stone, scatters inside, and leaves elsewhere; this is the soft, glowing look (subsurface scattering; skin, marble, wax, and milk behave the same way).

The sentence to remember: in a medium, at every point along a ray, light can be absorbed, scattered, or emitted, so radiance changes continuously along the ray.
